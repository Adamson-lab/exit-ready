# Regulatory crosswalk (starting point for Risk/Legal — not legal advice)

| Expectation | Where it comes from | What exit-ready gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Documented, **tested** exit strategy for ICT services supporting critical or important functions | DORA (Reg. (EU) 2022/2554) Art. 28(8) | `exit-plan.md` runbook + annual exit-test checklist; questionnaire domain *People & Testing* |
| Contractual exit/transition rights with an adequate transition period | DORA Art. 30(3)(f) | Questionnaire GOV-2, GOV-3 (gate questions) |
| **Stressed** and **non-stressed** exit planning | PRA SS2/21 (UK) | The two built-in scenarios: `stressed` and `planned` |
| Exit plans for critical outsourcing; substitutability assessment | EBA Outsourcing Guidelines (EBA/GL/2019/02) | Catalog target mapping per service; GOV-6 |
| Termination/exit planning as part of third-party lifecycle | US Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships (2023) | Cost-to-leave, timeline and feasibility verdict for committee papers |
| Customer's right to switch provider; switching charges being phased out | EU Data Act (Reg. (EU) 2023/2854) | `egress_waived` setting to model the waived-fee case — confirm applicability and dates with counsel |
| Data residency / cross-border transfer limits | National data-protection law; sector regulators (e.g. in Nigeria, check CBN requirements and the NDPC/NDPA regime) | DAT-5 question; target-environment mapping |

Use this table to brief your GRC team. It maps *where the evidence comes from*; whether your organisation complies is a judgement for your regulator-facing teams.
